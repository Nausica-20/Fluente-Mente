# Speak Without Translating

Ridurre la traduzione mentale e aumentare la velocità con cui si formulano risposte in inglese.

## 1. I understand English but cannot speak it

Identify the gap between recognition and active production and choose practical ways to close it.

## 2. How to stop translating in your head when speaking English

Use chunks, retrieval and familiar sentence frames to respond more directly.

## 3. Conversational chunks in English

Recognise useful multi-word chunks and understand why they make speech more natural.

## 4. Why knowing individual English words is not enough

Distinguish individual-word knowledge from knowledge of usable word combinations.

## 5. Essential collocations for everyday conversation

Use a practical set of high-frequency conversational collocations.

## 6. How to practise speaking English alone

Use simple solo speaking activities that create active output.

## 7. How to keep a conversation going in English

Use follow-up techniques to keep a simple conversation moving.

## 8. Short natural responses in English

Use short responses to show interest, agreement, surprise or hesitation.
