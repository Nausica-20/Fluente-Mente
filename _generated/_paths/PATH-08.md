# Everyday English Problem Solver

Gestire in inglese situazioni impreviste, errori e richieste di soluzione.

## 1. How to ask for help in English

Make clear and polite requests for help.

## 2. Polite requests in English

Choose natural structures for polite everyday requests.

## 3. How to ask for clarification in English

Use natural clarification questions in conversation and work situations.

## 4. How to say you do not understand in English

Use several natural ways to signal non-understanding and recover the conversation.

## 5. What to say when something goes wrong in English

Describe what went wrong, ask for help and check the solution.

## 6. How to complain politely in English

Describe a problem, state what you need and keep the interaction constructive.
