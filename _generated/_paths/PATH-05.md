# Conversation Skills

Partecipare a una conversazione in inglese dall’apertura alla chiusura senza dipendere da frasi memorizzate.

## 1. Natural greetings in English

Choose greetings appropriate to everyday social situations.

## 2. Starting a conversation in English

Use several natural conversation openers in common situations.

## 3. Follow-up questions in English

Build follow-up questions from what the other person just said.

## 4. Short natural responses in English

Use short responses to show interest, agreement, surprise or hesitation.

## 5. How to keep a conversation going in English

Use follow-up techniques to keep a simple conversation moving.

## 6. How to change the subject naturally in English

Use natural transition phrases to change topic.

## 7. How to give your opinion in English

Introduce and support an opinion using natural everyday phrases.

## 8. How to agree in English naturally

Use varied, natural ways to agree in conversation.

## 9. How to disagree politely in English

Use softening language when disagreeing in social and work conversations.
