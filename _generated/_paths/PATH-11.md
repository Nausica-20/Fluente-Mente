# Build a Sustainable English Routine

Creare una routine realistica che permetta di mantenere continuità e progressione.

## 1. Studying English without making progress

Identify common causes of stalled progress and choose a more targeted routine.

## 2. How much English should you study each day?

Set expectations and choose a study load that can be sustained.

## 3. Short sessions vs long study sessions for English

Match study-session length to goals, schedule and consistency.

## 4. How to build a realistic English study routine

Build a repeatable routine across speaking, listening, vocabulary and review.

## 5. How to review English without getting bored

Use varied retrieval-based review strategies.

## 6. Why you keep forgetting English vocabulary

Adopt retrieval and contextual review strategies instead of rereading alone.

Follow a repeatable short listening routine with measurable progression.
